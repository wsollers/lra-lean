# as-17 — DistributiveLattice

**STATUS: done, 2026-08-26.**

## Spec (given by the user)

Algebraic: a lattice where `x∧(y∨z)=(x∧y)∨(x∧z)` — and, by lattice duality,
this holds iff the dual `x∨(y∧z)=(x∨y)∧(x∨z)` holds too (either implies the
other given the lattice axioms). Order-theoretic/structural
characterizations given for context (Birkhoff's `M3`/`N5` forbidden-sublattice
theorem, uniqueness of complements, join-irreducible/join-prime equivalence)
and representation theorems (Birkhoff's finite representation via down-sets
of join-irreducibles, Priestley/Stone duality) — the latter two sections are
explicitly deep structural/representation theory, well beyond audit/reorg/
example scope, and not attempted (same category as `CommutativeMonoid`'s
divisibility-preorder or `Group`'s cancellativity notes — consequences to
know about, not new axioms to build). Standard examples: power sets, down-set
lattices of a poset, chains, divisor lattices `(ℕ_{>0}, lcm, gcd)`.

## Audit

`DistributiveLatticeLaws join meet := LatticeLaws join meet ∧
TwoSidedDistributive join meet ∧ TwoSidedDistributive meet join` — states
**both** distributive identities (join-distributes-over-meet *and*
meet-distributes-over-join) as separate conjuncts, even though the user's own
spec notes these are equivalent given the lattice axioms (only one needs to
be an axiom).

This is the mirror image of `BoundedLattice`'s finding (as-16): that item
*under*-specified correctly by relying on a derivable consequence; this one
*over*-specifies by stating a derivable consequence explicitly anyway.
Confirmed this is **not a bug** — just a deliberate non-minimal redundancy
matching this tree's general style of stating two-sided properties directly
rather than chasing a minimal axiom set (`TwoSidedIdentity`/`TwoSidedInverse`
do the same thing under commutativity elsewhere in this tree). No fix
needed; documented the equivalence explicitly in the new doc comment so a
future reader doesn't mistake it for an oversight.
