**Bookkeeping note (not a math issue):** the chapter `\lrameta` labels have a numbering
collision — `completeness` and `functions` are both stamped "Chapter 4," and every chapter
from `elementary-functions` onward is stamped one number behind where continuous numbering
across the whole volume would put it (`elementary-functions`="Chapter 8" collides with
`function-sequences`="Chapter 8"; `continuity`=9, `differentiation`=10, `integration`=11, each
one low). Looks like `completeness`'s promotion to its own chapter shifted everything after it
without the labels being renumbered. Cosmetic, but worth a cleanup pass whenever convenient.

**Biggest finding from building this roadmap:** function limits (the ε-δ definition of
`lim_{x→c}f(x)=L`) — the thing we went looking for back in the Functions chapter and couldn't
find — live in **Book II, Chapter 9 (Continuity), section `limits`**, not in `real-analysis` as
guessed earlier, and not in `functions`/`subsets-real-line` (which only laid scaffolding toward
it). `real-analysis` (Chapter 1) turns out to be proof-technique/methods content (asymptotic
notation, inequalities, error propagation, `|·|`), not limit theory at all.

**Second finding:** `structure-of-real-line` (Chapter 2, not yet audited) looks like it heavily
overlaps `functions/subsets-real-line` (already audited) — both cover open/closed sets,
interior/exterior/boundary, limit/isolated points. Worth checking for duplication vs.
deliberate two-pass treatment (concrete-ℝ-only vs. topological-space-flavored) when we get there.
