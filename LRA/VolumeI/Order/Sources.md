# Order Formalization Sources

The Volume I order formalization will follow:

- *Introduction to Lattices and Order* by B. A. Davey and H. A. Priestley.
- *Ordered Sets* by Bernd S. W. Schroder.
- The LRA omnibus, Chapters 0.3--0.4 and 4, especially Definitions 0.7 and
  0.9, Theorems 0.8 and 0.10, and Definition 4.1. These are the LaTeX-side
  counterparts of the strict/non-strict order and completeness interfaces and
  must remain synchronized with this formalization.
