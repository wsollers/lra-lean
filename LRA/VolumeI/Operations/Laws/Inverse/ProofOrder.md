# Inverse Laws Proof Order

## Prerequisites

- [ ] Identity laws are defined.
- [ ] Identity projection theorems are proved.
- [ ] Identity coincidence and uniqueness are proved.
- [ ] Associativity is defined.
- [ ] Reassociation theorems needed for inverse proofs are proved.

## Definitions

- [ ] Define left inverse element.
- [ ] Define right inverse element.
- [ ] Define two-sided inverse element.
- [ ] Define invertible element if needed.

## Theorems

- [ ] A left inverse and right inverse of the same element coincide under the
      needed associativity and identity hypotheses.
- [ ] Two-sided inverses are unique under the needed associativity and identity
      hypotheses.
- [ ] Left cancellation follows from a suitable left inverse.
- [ ] Right cancellation follows from a suitable right inverse.

## Learning Checks

- [ ] Explain where associativity is used in each inverse proof.
- [ ] Explain why identity alone is not enough for cancellation.
- [ ] Explain why inverse language depends on the selected identity element.
