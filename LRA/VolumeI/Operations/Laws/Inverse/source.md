# Sources For Inverse Laws

## Landau

The first two chapters of Landau motivate inverse-like reasoning mainly through
subtraction and division as solvability statements after order and operation
laws are available.

For this generic topic, do not begin with division. Begin with:

- operation;
- identity;
- associativity;
- inverse element.

Then prove cancellation and uniqueness generically.
