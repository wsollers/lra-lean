# Inverse Laws Topic

Inverse laws come after identity and associativity. They should not be taught
as a magic cancellation button.

## Core Questions

- What is a left inverse relative to an identity?
- What is a right inverse relative to an identity?
- What is a two-sided inverse?
- Why does inverse uniqueness need associativity?
- How do identity and associativity combine to prove cancellation?

## Dependency Warning

Do not implement inverse theorems before the identity topic is stable.

Many inverse arguments need all three ingredients:

- an identity element;
- associativity;
- an inverse law.
