# Sources For Closure

## Landau

Landau's first chapters constantly enforce closure, even when not phrased with
that modern word:

- natural-number addition returns a natural number;
- natural-number multiplication returns a natural number;
- fraction operations return fractions;
- rational-number operations return rational numbers after passing to
  equivalence classes;
- embedded integers remain closed under addition and multiplication.

This is the right motivation for making closure visible before the later laws.

## Boundary With Well-Definedness

Closure and well-definedness are related but distinct.

Closure asks whether the result lands in the intended carrier.

Well-definedness asks whether a definition does not depend on a representative
choice.

Volume II quotient constructions often need both:

1. the representative-level operation produces a valid representative;
2. equivalent representatives produce equivalent outputs.
