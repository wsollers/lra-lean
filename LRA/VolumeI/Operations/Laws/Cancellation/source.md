# Sources For Cancellation

## Landau

Landau's early arithmetic proofs repeatedly use cancellation-style reasoning,
especially when proving uniqueness, order reflection, and solvability results.

For this generic topic, cancellation should be abstracted as a law of an
arbitrary operation and later certified by concrete systems.

## Current Cutoff

Do not implement cancellation before identity, associativity, and inverse
relationships are audited.
