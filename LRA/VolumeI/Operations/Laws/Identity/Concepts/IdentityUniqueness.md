# Identity Uniqueness

## Informal Meaning

Two two-sided identities for the same operation are equal.

This follows from identity coincidence: one identity supplies a left identity,
the other supplies a right identity, and left/right identities coincide.

## Learning Point

Uniqueness of identity still does not require associativity. The uniqueness
proof is about the two-sided behavior of the identity element, not about
reparenthesizing products.
