# Sources For Identity Laws

## Landau

Landau's first two chapters motivate this topic indirectly.

Chapter I constructs natural-number addition and multiplication and proves the
laws that later behave like identity, associativity, commutativity, and
distributivity facts.

Chapter II constructs fractions, proves well-definedness under equivalence, and
then promotes equivalence classes to rational numbers. This supports the
repository split:

- Volume I: generic laws of operations;
- Volume II: concrete systems and certifications of those laws.

## Current Cutoff

For this identity topic, do not pull in fraction equivalence, quotient
