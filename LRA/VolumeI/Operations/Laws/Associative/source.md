# Sources For Associativity

## Landau

Landau Chapter I proves associativity of natural-number addition and
multiplication after constructing those operations.

For this topic, Landau motivates the learning sequence:

1. define a concrete operation;
2. prove associativity concretely;
3. abstract the law;
4. reuse the abstract law when later systems certify it.

## Current Cutoff

This topic should not yet prove concrete natural-number or rational-number
associativity. Those belong in Volume II certifications.
