# Sign And Negation Proof Order

## Prerequisite Contracts

- [ ] Record the additive identity contract.
- [ ] Record the additive inverse contract.
- [ ] Record zero absorbing for multiplication.
- [ ] Record distributivity of multiplication over addition.
- [ ] Record subtraction as addition of negation.

## Direct Theorems

- [ ] Prove additive inverse involution.
- [ ] Prove multiplication by negative one on the left.
- [ ] Prove multiplication by negative one on the right where appropriate.
- [ ] Prove multiplication by a negated right factor.
- [ ] Prove multiplication by a negated left factor.
- [ ] Prove product of two negated factors.
- [ ] Prove distributivity over subtraction.

## Relationship Theorems

- [ ] Show which sign laws require distributivity.
- [ ] Show which sign laws require only additive inverse uniqueness.
- [ ] Show commutativity transfers left sign laws to right sign laws.

## Learning Checks

- [ ] Negation is additive inverse language, not order language by itself.
- [ ] Negative multiplication order reversal requires order hypotheses.
- [ ] Product-of-negatives is not an axiom once the algebraic structure is
      strong enough.
