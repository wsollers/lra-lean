# Sources For Order Compatibility

## Landau

Landau Chapter I proves order interaction with natural-number addition and
multiplication. Chapter II repeats the pattern for fractions and rational
numbers after well-definedness under equivalence is established.

This generic topic abstracts the operation-order part of that pattern.

## Boundary

Concrete proofs that natural, fraction, or rational operations preserve order
belong in Volume II. This topic owns the generic vocabulary and consequences.
