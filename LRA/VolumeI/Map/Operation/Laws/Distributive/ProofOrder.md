# Distributivity Proof Order

## Definitions

- [ ] Define left distributivity.
- [ ] Define right distributivity.
- [ ] Define two-sided distributivity.

## Direct Theorems

- [ ] Apply left distributivity.
- [ ] Apply right distributivity.
- [ ] Project left distributivity from two-sided distributivity.
- [ ] Project right distributivity from two-sided distributivity.
- [ ] Assemble two-sided distributivity from left and right distributivity.

## Relationship Theorems

- [ ] Commutative outer operation turns left distributivity into right
      distributivity.
- [ ] Commutative outer operation turns right distributivity into left
      distributivity.
- [ ] Distributivity plus additive identity can prove absorbing behavior in
      richer algebraic settings, when the needed cancellation or inverse
      hypotheses are available.

## Learning Checks

- [ ] Distributivity is about two operations, not one.
- [ ] Left and right distributivity are different without commutativity.
- [ ] Distributivity does not imply associativity of either operation.
- [ ] Distributivity does not imply commutativity of either operation.
