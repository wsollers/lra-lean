# Commutativity Proof Order

## Definitions

- [ ] Define commutativity for a binary endo-operation.

## Direct Theorems

- [ ] Apply commutativity to swap two inputs.
- [ ] Use commutativity in both directions without changing the mathematical
      statement.

## Relationship Theorems

- [ ] Left identity plus commutativity gives right identity.
- [ ] Right identity plus commutativity gives left identity.
- [ ] Left absorbing plus commutativity gives right absorbing.
- [ ] Right absorbing plus commutativity gives left absorbing.
- [ ] One-sided distributivity implies the other side when the relevant
      operation is commutative.

## Learning Checks

- [ ] Commutativity does not imply associativity.
- [ ] Commutativity does not imply existence of an identity.
- [ ] Commutativity does not imply cancellation.
