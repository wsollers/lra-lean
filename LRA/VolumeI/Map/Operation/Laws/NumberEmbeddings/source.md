# Number Embeddings And Mixed Operations Sources

This topic is internal project scaffolding.

The motivating examples come from the Volume II tower of concrete number
systems: naturals into integers, integers into rationals, rationals into reals,
and reals into complex numbers. The Volume I role is to isolate the generic
concept of embedding-preserved operations and mixed-carrier result ownership.

Preserving a binary operation alone is intentionally weak. For example, a
constant map into a neutral or idempotent point may preserve a binary operation
without being an embedding. Real number-system embeddings must also preserve
distinguished elements and, once the embedding concept is implemented, must be
injective.
